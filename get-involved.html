LASAL Impact Summit

Education as a Social Mobility — September 2026 · Lagos, Nigeria

The LASAL Impact Summit marks 10 years of sustained intervention in supporting students through financial barriers to education and expanding access to tertiary opportunities. It is convened to reflect on the Foundation's impact, celebrate beneficiaries and alumni, and strengthen connections across its growing community of scholars and partners. Beyond reflection, the summit serves as a strategic platform to consolidate lessons, deepen engagement with stakeholders across Nigeria and the diaspora, and mobilize support for the next phase of scale, sustainability, and greater educational impact.
