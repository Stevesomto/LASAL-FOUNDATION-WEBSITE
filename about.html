About Our Foundation

Committed to Educational Equity & Excellence

Founded in 2015 by Alhaji Chief Lateef Ajibade Salami, LASAL Foundation has been bridging the divide between raw talent and economic opportunity for students worldwide.

Our Core Mission & Vision

We believe that financial hardship should never be a barrier to academic brilliance. Our vision is a world where every determined student, regardless of background, can access higher education and cultivate their full potential.

The LASAL Foundation has awarded 45 scholarships since 2015. To date, 31 beneficiaries have graduated successfully, and 14 are currently pursuing their studies under the Foundation's auspices.

THE LATEEF AJIBADE SALAMI FOUNDATION was incorporated in IKEJA, Nigeria with Registration Number 80023. It was registered on 12th August 2015 and it's current status is unknown. Company's registered office address is SUITE 3, CANAAN PLAZA, PLOT 2 BLOCK 105, LEKKI PHASE 1 LEKKI EXPRESSWAY
