Alumni Review

Gbonjubola Yusuff Tunde

Physiotherapist | Researcher | LASAL Foundation Alumnus

My LASAL Foundation Journey: A Legacy of Impact and Lifelong Support

I came to know about the LASAL Foundation during my 200-level studies at the University of Ibadan through the Youth Leader of my town. I applied for the scholarship and was privileged to be among the Foundation's first set of beneficiaries.

At the time, the scholarship was a tremendous relief, easing the financial burden on both my parents and me. It covered my tuition fees, accommodation, and a monthly stipend throughout my university education, allowing me to pursue my studies with greater focus and peace of mind.

Beyond financial support, LASAL became a second family to me. Through its well-structured mentorship programme, I received guidance, encouragement, and direction throughout my academic journey. The Chairperson and Board of Trustees were consistently accessible, always a call or message away whenever I needed support. The Foundation's sponsored physical mentorship sessions, which brought in experienced mentors and experts to train beneficiaries at the head office, also helped us begin planning purposefully for life beyond graduation.

Today, approximately seven years after my graduation, I remain a proud alumnus of the LASAL Foundation. As a physiotherapist, researcher, and professional committed to continuous learning and service, I continue to appreciate the values, opportunities, and support the Foundation invested in me. What makes LASAL particularly remarkable is that its commitment to beneficiaries does not end at graduation; it recognises that education, personal development, and mentorship are lifelong journeys.

LASAL is more than a scholarship foundation. It is a purposeful initiative, a community, and a family that invests in people and their potential. I am deeply grateful to the Chairperson and Board of Trustees for their unwavering commitment, generosity, and continued support.

I pray that Almighty God continues to grant the Chairperson, Board of Trustees, mentors, and the entire LASAL family wisdom, strength, good health, and abundant resources to sustain and expand this noble mission. May the Foundation continue to transform lives, empower generations, and inspire its beneficiaries to become responsible contributors to society.

"The true measure of an investment in education is not only the success of the beneficiary, but the lives that beneficiary goes on to transform."

To LASAL Foundation, thank you for believing in us, investing in our future, and reminding us that meaningful support can become a lifelong legacy.

Alumni Review

Anthonia Abah

Chemical / Process Engineer | LASAL Foundation Alumnus

A gift that keeps giving

I am Anthonia Abah, a Chemical Engineering graduate from the Federal University of Technology Minna, Niger State, Nigeria.

My friend, Abel Onuh, sent me the link to the LASAL Foundation scholarship, as he always does with sharing opportunities with me. I took action as usual and tried to submit from my end, but I was having issues with submission, so I asked Abel to help me submit from his end too to ensure my application was submitted. Amid the hurdles with the very bad network then in Gidan Kwano, my school campus, which has gotten a great deal of intervention, by the way, I was able to pull through with the other requirements and steps of the application process. Thankfully, the program officer then was very patient with me too. I thought I would lose the opportunity at some point, but thankfully I didn't.

I was still very fresh into school, navigating and finding my path in my second year at the university, and had also recently lost my Dad. I was still processing the loss while having to ensure I succeeded academically. So I would say LASAL came just in time; it felt like having a new community and family, especially with getting access to the chairperson in addition to me not having to worry about school fees and accommodation fees and even getting additional monthly stipends.

Becoming a LASAL beneficiary is/was one of the gifts of education to me; I was getting support to learn and fare very well in school ? financially and otherwise, as I had the opportunity to be mentored by the chairperson too. I learnt a lot of other life skills, etiquette, and even financial literacy in addition to the many inspirations I got/get indirectly from her and the other board members.

Looking back at young Anthonia in year two, who was still finding her feet in school, and now, I am and will always be grateful for the impact of LASAL Foundation in my personal and professional growth. For me, it was not just getting supported financially, but all round. I have gone further to impact and mentor others reaching over 3000 students both directly and indirectly, gotten awards, certifications, building my online presence, very financially literate and still growing rapidly in that regard. Very importantly, I graduated as Chemical Engineer with Second Class Upper Division Honours with CGPA of ~80%. Did I mention getting a life handbook in the form of a book written by the Chairperson ? A Conscious Life; Navigating Critical Stages and Aspects of Life Successfully? To say the least, that book is a personal best seller which I continue to go back to from time to time and have mummy speak to me indirectly?

I am currently a graduate Chemical/Process Engineer, honing my skills and getting experience at the intersection of energy systems, process engineering, data-driven optimisation, and sustainable industrial development.

Thank you LASAL Foundation for always. Truly, a gift that keeps giving.

ABAYOMI OYELEYE

Coming from a family of eight, Abayomi's education was threatened when his father lost his job, leaving his mother as the family's main source of income. Paying his university fees became a major challenge, and he feared he might not complete his studies. LASAL Foundation provided tuition support and a monthly stipend, allowing him to focus on his academics. He graduated with First Class honours and as the Best Graduating Student in his department. Today, he is pursuing his PhD in Portugal.

GENESIS ADEWUYI

Before LASAL Foundation, Genesis struggled financially to pursue her dream of becoming a veterinarian. After spending an extra year working to raise money for school and continuing with menial jobs after gaining admission, the demands of veterinary school made it difficult to balance work and her studies. LASAL Foundation provided the support she needed, covering her tuition, accommodation and monthly expenses, while also investing in her personal development through mentorship. The Foundation's support gave her the stability to focus on her education and shaped her outlook on life. Today, Genesis has completed her Doctor of Veterinary Medicine degree, finished her NYSC, and is practising as a freelance veterinarian, with a particular interest in avian medicine. She describes LASAL as a place that helped turn her dream into reality.
