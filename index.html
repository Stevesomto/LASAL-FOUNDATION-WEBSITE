The Challenge

18% of students in Nigerian tertiary institutions drop out due to financial constraints (1 in every 5 students).

Over 2.5 million students are enrolled in Nigerian tertiary institutions; 450,000 student dreams of earning a degree are threatened due to financial challenges.

Why LASAL Exists…

Inspired by the Founder's father, a firm advocate and believer in education as a tool for social mobility and established in remembrance of beloved brothers.

Our Belief…

  • Education transforms lives, families, communities, and nations.
  • LASAL invests in people, not just scholarships.
