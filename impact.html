Recognised By

Osun State University (UNIOSUN)

LASAL Foundation is listed on UNIOSUN’s Financial Aid & Scholarships page as one of the sponsors supporting its students, alongside national programmes such as NELFUND and the NNPC joint-venture scholarships. Several LASAL scholars study at UNIOSUN, and several of our alumni studied there.

“A non-governmental organization founded in memory of Late Alhaji Chief Lateef Ajibade Salami. Dedicated to promoting social mobility and empowering indigent, brilliant tertiary students.” UNIOSUN, Financial Aid & Scholarships
